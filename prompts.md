# My prompt

Prompt 1 

Read README.md in this repo. Build a polished, mobile-friendly one-page
website (index.html, style.css, and a small script.js if needed) for
international students at NDHU who do not speak Chinese.

CONTENT RULES (most important):
- Use ONLY the facts in README.md. Do not add or change any prices, times,
  bus numbers, addresses, phone numbers, dates, or place names. Keep my
  wording; you may only fix obvious typos.
- Keep every link from README.md and make it clickable.
- If any line in README.md contains a bracketed note like [CHECK: ...],
  show it with a small "Unverified" badge and keep the note text.
- After you finish, list in your reply anything on the page that is not
  directly from README.md, so I can review it.

STRUCTURE:
- A hero header with the title and one short line saying who the guide
  is for (international students at NDHU).
- A sticky top menu with links to all five sections. On mobile it should
  collapse into a simple menu button.
- One card-style section per heading, with the section number visible.
- Show the airport-to-NDHU route as a clear step-by-step layout, with
  Route A and Route B side by side on desktop and stacked on mobile.
- Show the contact details for Mrs. Jenny in a separate highlighted box.
- Show tables (bus passes, food places, course dates) as clean, readable
  tables that scroll sideways on small screens.
- In the food table, add a "Copy" button next to each Chinese name so a
  student can paste it into Google Maps. Also add a Google Maps search
  link for each Chinese name.

DESIGN:
- Modern, clean, and calm: one accent color (a blue or teal), plenty of
  white space, rounded cards, and a readable font (system font stack, no
  external downloads).
- Readable text size (at least 16px), strong contrast, and large tap
  targets for mobile.
- Include a "Back to top" button.
- Add a simple footer: "Written by a student at NDHU. Built with AI
  assistance. Prices, times, and dates may change, so check official
  sources."
- English only. No images from the internet, no external libraries.
