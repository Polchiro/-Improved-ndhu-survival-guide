1. Arriving at NDHU

Getting from Taoyuan Airport to NDHU

1

Taoyuan Airport to Taipei Main Station

Take the Airport MRT (NT$160).

2

Taipei Main Station to the Hualien area

Take a Taiwan Railway (TRA) train. Timetable: https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip?lang=EN_US

3

Choose one of two routes:

Option A
Route A

Get off at Hualien Station, then take Bus 301 (NT$59) to NDHU. You can check approximate bus times in a Taiwan bus app. A taxi costs about NT$400-500, which is quite expensive.

Option B
Route B

Get off at Zhixue Station. Walking to NDHU is 1.5 km, which is not recommended with luggage. A taxi costs about NT$150, but few drivers accept this short trip. You can book your train ticket from Taipei to Zhixue Station in the OSA Limited Shuttle Bus Timetable app before you come to Taiwan, so you can simply use it on your travel date.

🛏️ What I wish I'd known

For Dorm VII, you need to bring or buy your own bedding: a fitted bedsheet and mattress for a 194 × 82 cm bed, a pillow with a pillowcase, and a blanket. You can buy them at the supermarket outside the campus.

⚠️ What stressed me the most

Walking to the dorm. The campus is huge, I was carrying two suitcases, and I did not know where the dorm was until my freshman leader sent me the location. I recommend that you contact your freshman leader or Mrs. Jenny beforehand, so you won't wander around carrying your suitcases.

Important Contact

Contact: Mrs. Jenny, international student support

  • Office: Student Activities Division, Office of Student Affairs, Room C102, Student Activity Center, NDHU
  • Address: No. 1, Sec. 2, University Road, Shoufeng Township, Hualien County
  • Phone: 03-890-6209 (from abroad: +886-3-890-6209)
  • Website: osa.ndhu.edu.tw

This office helps with administrative support, admission and registration orientations, residence permits (ARC) and work permits, and international reception volunteers.

Tips

Your ARC (Alien Resident Certificate)

  • Apply as soon as possible on this website: https://coa.immigration.gov.tw/coa-frontend/student/entry?lang=en
  • Make sure you have mobile data on your phone so you can find the right place to collect your ARC. If you don't have mobile data yet, prepare clear directions beforehand, or go with a friend instead of going alone.
  • Collect your ARC at the NIA Hualien County Service Center:
    • Address: 5F, No. 371, Zhongshan Road, Hualien City, Hualien County 970009, Taiwan
    • Opening hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-5:00 PM (open through lunchtime)

Bus passes

If you plan to use the bus often, buy an EasyCard and then sign up for a monthly bus pass at 7-Eleven. A monthly pass is cheaper than paying for each ride. Each pass is valid for 30 days and gives unlimited rides within its coverage. There are two options:

Pass Price Coverage
Hualien Basic Monthly Pass NT$199 Eligible Hualien local buses, plus Taiwan Railway (TRA) between Heping and Fuli stations on eligible services
Hualien Extended Monthly Pass NT$399 Everything in the Basic Pass, plus eligible intercity and highway bus routes within Hualien County and selected Hualien-Taitung services

2. Phone plan

I recommend signing up for the NT$600-per-month contract with Chunghwa Telecom (in my opinion, one of the best telecom companies in Taiwan) on Registration Day. It is a good opportunity to get mobile data without an ARC. If you miss it, you must wait until you receive your ARC and then sign up in the city. Without mobile data, collecting my ARC alone in the city was very difficult.

3. Dorm life

Prepare cleaning tools, and agree on a cleaning schedule with your roommates. Your room is where you live and study the most, so keeping it clean matters.

4. Food and groceries

If you want to cook

Buy food outside the campus, near or inside a supermarket. It is quite a bit cheaper than ready-made food from Family Mart.

Recommended supermarket: Shoufeng Township Farmers' Association Supermarket, Zhixue Store.

Search for it on Google Maps with this name: 壽豐鄉農會超市 志學門市

📍 Search on Google Maps

Address: No. 62, Section 3, Zhongshan Road

If you don't want to cook

Buy a meal box from Fried Chicken Master or the other sellers beside it. They sell a variety of foods. I recommend a meal box with vegetables, egg, and the food you want, so you get enough protein and vitamins. Since you will eat there long-term, try not to eat only fried food.

All the places below are on the 1st floor of the Ji-Xian Building (集賢館), near Dorms V and VI, a short walk from Dorm VII. To find one on Google Maps, search its Chinese name.
